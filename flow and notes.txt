 METHOD:

1) read actual walking csv from phone, take accz from it 
2) apply low pass butterworth on accz
3) normalize using Z Score
4) take xcorr of norm_signal to find the CNT (stride length)
5) using CNT, we can detect steps by taking CNT sized chunks of samples from the norm_signal
6) compare the similarity of the step_count[i-1] with steps_count[i] using xcorr. Store them in a array
7) if ACF > thershold NW or else UW
8) F1 score
